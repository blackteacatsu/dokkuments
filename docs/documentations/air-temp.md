## Description
Air temperature represents the average temperature of the air near the Earth's surface. This variable is crucial for understanding energy balance, evapotranspiration rates, and overall climate conditions in the Amazon basin.

## Specification
Labeled as **Tair_f_tavg**, representing the time-averaged air temperature.

**Unit: $\degree\text{C}$ (degrees Celsius)**

## Applications
- Climate monitoring and trend analysis
- Evapotranspiration modeling
- Agricultural planning and forecasting
- Ecosystem health assessment
