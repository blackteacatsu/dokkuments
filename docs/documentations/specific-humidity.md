## Description
Specific humidity measures the mass of water vapor present in a unit mass of air. This variable is essential for understanding atmospheric moisture content, which affects evapotranspiration, precipitation formation, and overall water cycle dynamics in the Amazon basin.

## Specification
Labeled as **Qair_f_tavg**, representing the time-averaged specific humidity.

**Unit: $g/kg$ (grams of water vapor per kilogram of air)**

## Applications
- Evapotranspiration estimation
- Atmospheric moisture transport analysis
- Weather forecasting
- Climate modeling
- Understanding convective processes
