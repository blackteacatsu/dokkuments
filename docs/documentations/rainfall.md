## Description
Precipitation represents the amount of rainfall falling over a specific area per unit time. This variable is fundamental for understanding water availability, flood risk, and hydrological cycles in the Amazon basin.

## Specification
Labeled as **Rainf_tavg**, representing the time-averaged rainfall rate.

**Unit: $mm/\text{day}$ (millimeters per day)**

## Applications
- Flood forecasting and early warning systems
- Agricultural water management
- Hydrological modeling
- Climate change impact assessment
- Water resource planning
